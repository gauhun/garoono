## Audience

Technical or technical curious, time poor, budget conscious, allergic to hype
They want what works for one person after hours, not enterprise playbooks

Casual, first person, direct, like talking to another builder

Short lines, lots of whitespace, practical takeaway first

## House style (enforced by `npm run blog:lint`)

- One sentence per line, and no full stop at the end of a sentence
- Blank line between paragraphs
- No em dash or en dash anywhere, use a comma or a new line
- No filler or AI tells: game-changer, dive in, delve, leverage, seamless, robust, elevate, harness, realm, tapestry, furthermore, moreover, in this article, in conclusion, the world of, unlock your potential, supercharge, look no further
- Every stat (%, million, billion, crore, lakh) carries a source link on the same line, to the primary source
- 600+ words, ## section headings, a table or checklist, and 1 or 2 inline SVG figures

## Do

- Lead with the takeaway a solo builder can act on tonight
- Anchor claims in official docs (Apple, Google, Firebase) or named research
- Frame everything through time, money and doing it alone

## Don't

- Fabricate stats, quotes, case studies or personal stories
- Write for VC funded teams
- Sound like a press release or a listicle farm
